# Story bank

Fictional demo persona.

## 1. Cutting churn with a model people trusted

- Evidence: LKM-01, LKM-03
- Context: The subscription team could not tell which accounts were about to cancel.
- Action: Built a churn-risk model in Python and SQL and reviewed it weekly with the team.
- Result: Monthly churn fell 18% for the 2024 cohort versus 2023.
- Useful for: impact, stakeholder management, modelling questions.
- Short draft: "I built a churn-risk model the subscription team checked every Monday; churn for the 2024 cohort fell 18%."
- Do not invent: revenue impact or team size.

## 2. Making reports fast enough to use

- Evidence: LKM-02, HBH-02
- Context: Revenue dashboards took six hours to refresh, so people exported spreadsheets instead.
- Action: Rebuilt the models in dbt and the dashboards in Tableau.
- Result: Refresh time fell from 6 hours to 20 minutes.
- Useful for: efficiency, tooling, data-engineering-adjacent roles.
- Short draft: "The revenue dashboards went from a six-hour refresh to twenty minutes, so people stopped exporting spreadsheets."
- Do not invent: cost savings.
