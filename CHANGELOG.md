# Changelog

All notable changes to The Jobfather. Your own learning history lives in your workspace
(`learning/CHANGELOG.md`), not here.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-19)

First public version.

- Onboarding that imports a CV, LinkedIn export and writing samples, drafts the profile from
  facts only and fills gaps with a short interview.
- Market map for any field: title graph, seniority, skills with evidence, companies, locations,
  platforms, saved searches, exclusions and scoring.
- Job intake, tracker workbook (keeps your edits), ranking, company research cache, mailbox reply
  tracking, action brief and follow-up timing.
- One-page LaTeX CV rendered from the profile, tailored per job with full PDF validation and a
  visual review before saving; saved starting points per role family.
- Writing in the user's voice: default rules, vendored humanizer checklist, avoid-list lint,
  real email signature handling.
- Applications with policy modes, per-item approvals and a human final click: Greenhouse autofill
  with safety rules, guided filling for other systems.
- Guided logins for the browser, LinkedIn and mail on Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Antigravity.
- Naukri support (an independent, clean-room server) follows in 0.2.0.
- A per-user change journal with rollback, logged outside edits, a pending-learning queue,
  learning from sent-email edits, and a weekly review.
- Built-in lessons from real use: platform filters are unreliable, a snippet is not a JD, a bounce
  is not a closed job, never guess contacts, never widen the date window to hit a number.
